How it ends, from today

Each simulation raises round after round while it clears the bar; otherwise it is acquired (probability and price "est.") or shuts down. At the last round, it exits at a random multiple of the last post-money, or fails. Proceeds go through the preference waterfall, then tax.

Limitations

  • No down rounds with anti-dilution, no secondary sales, pool treated as common stock.
  • Simplified tax: a single rate on proceeds, ignoring French holding-company rollover (apport-cession), BSPCE rules and the automatic high-income surtax.
  • This is neither legal nor tax advice: have the shareholders' agreement and tax treatment reviewed by a lawyer and a tax advisor.
